# v0.12.9 — 2025 environment back-test (5,000 mocks)

## Executive conclusion

- Primary grade-to-realized optimal-lineup correlation: **0.163**; skill-core sensitivity: **0.18**.
- Top preseason-grade decile averaged **1792.7** realized points versus **1705** for the bottom decile (difference **87.8**).
- No Chumps averaged **1828.1** realized optimal points versus **1739.7** for the field (difference **88.5**).
- No Chumps preseason grade: first 50.6%, top-three 85%; realized: first 21.1%, top-three 49.2%.
- Actual 2025 champion **URINE TROUBLE** had frozen-environment grade 81.4 (rank 5/9) and drafted-roster optimal score 1964.6.
- This is an ex-post behavioral-profile test: current full-history profiles, including 2025 behavior, are intentionally retained. No NFL-team affinity was added.

## Frozen 2025 environment

- 331 analysis-only players selected from the archived 2025 ADP market; 306 have at least one mapped weekly outcome row (absence is scored as realized zero, not backfilled).
- Player identity, position, NFL team, bye, and planning ADP are historical inputs. Embedded historical player names/order are cross-walked to these archived positions; the known misaligned position-sequence arrays are not used.
- Historical projections, situation/depth role, breakout, bust, contingent-RB, and exact tiers were unavailable. They are neutralized at 50 (tiers blank) in the primary arm.
- Tier sensitivity uses six-player within-position ADP bands only as a labeled proxy; it is not presented as archived tier truth.
- Actual outcomes are held outside the model VM and are scored only after the 170th pick.

## Mock design and runtime

- Primary: 5000 drafts, seeds 20255001–20260000, 2472 seconds. No Chumps plus nine opponents; all 17 historical opponent profiles and Neutral appear exactly 2500 times each. Cards randomized.
- Fixed 2025 room: 250 drafts, seeds 20265001–20265250, 120.8 seconds. Actual ten identities/cards; Cam + Guy inferred as the only missing Card 4 row.
- Tier sensitivity: 250 drafts, seeds 20275001–20275250, 124.3 seconds.
- Exact cascading replay skipped: the Cam + Guy draft row is missing, so the complete 170-pick historical board cannot be established.

## Bias and hindsight safeguards

- weekly realized player outcomes are absent from the model VM: **PASS**
- selection and grade function source contains no realized-outcome reference: **PASS**
- every outcome score is calculated only after a 170-pick completion assertion: **PASS**
- production file hashes are identical before and after the study: **PASS**

## Model weaknesses revealed

- Exact historical projections, situation/role inputs, breakout/bust inputs, and tiers are unavailable; several grade components are therefore neutral or market-only.
- Exact league K/D/ST scoring is unavailable. Skill-core sensitivity is included so special-teams proxy choices cannot silently drive the conclusion.
- Current owner profiles include 2025 behavior by design, making this an ex-post behavioral-profile back-test rather than point-in-time forecasting.
- Actual league totals include waiver, trade, and lineup management; drafted-roster optimal scoring does not.
- The grade is strongly market-derived in this reconstruction, so correlation partly measures whether preseason ADP predicted 2025 production.

## Conclusion

The reconstructed preseason grade has little relationship with actual 2025 drafted-roster scoring. Do not retune production from this study alone; recover stronger point-in-time inputs and exact scoring first.

No production file, owner profile, NFL-team affinity, recommendation coefficient, grade weight, player pool, hazard, live adaptation rule, export path, or PWA artifact was changed.
